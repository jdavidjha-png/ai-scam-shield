# Phishing basics

Phishing is a type of online scam where someone pretends to be a trusted organization. The goal is to trick you into sharing passwords, bank details, or one-time codes.

Common signs include urgent language, threats that your account will be closed, and links that do not match the real company domain. Scammers may use email, SMS, or social media messages.

If you are unsure, do not click links in the message. Open the official app or website yourself, or call the organization using a number from their official site—not from the suspicious message.

Legitimate companies rarely ask for your full password or payment card PIN by message. Treat any request for sensitive data as a red flag until you verify the sender through a separate trusted channel.
