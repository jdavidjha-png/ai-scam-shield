# Safe online habits

Never share passwords, one-time verification codes, government ID numbers, or full payment card details in chat, email, or over the phone with an unverified caller.

To verify a suspicious message, pause before you act. Check the sender address, look for spelling mistakes, and compare links carefully. When in doubt, contact the company directly using contact details you find on their official website.

Use strong unique passwords and turn on two-factor authentication where available. This limits damage if one account is compromised.

Be cautious with unexpected attachments or download links, even if they appear to come from someone you know. Accounts can be hijacked and used to spread malware or scams to contacts.
