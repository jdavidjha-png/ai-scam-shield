# Reporting scams

If you receive a scam message, do not reply or engage with the sender. Save screenshots or forward the message to your email provider’s abuse contact if applicable.

Report phishing to your bank or service provider if the message impersonated them. Many organizations maintain dedicated fraud or security email addresses listed on their official sites.

You can also report scams to local consumer protection or cybercrime authorities. Reporting helps investigators track patterns and warn others.

After reporting, block the sender and delete the message. If you clicked a link or shared information, change passwords immediately and monitor accounts for unusual activity.
